# Data Sharing MOU (Draft - Pilot)

Purpose: share product & batch metadata (no PHI) to enable batch-linked patient feedback.

Parties: Platform Co. (independent controller) & [Partner Pharmacy/Distributor] (controller).

Scope:
- Daily CSV/S3 drop of product catalog & batch numbers (SKU, name, form, THC/CBD, pack size, batch_no, expiry).
- No patient identifiers; no prescribing data.

Compliance:
- DPIA completed; GDPR-compliant processing; audit logs kept.
- Right to withdraw with 7 days' notice.

Term: 12 weeks (pilot).

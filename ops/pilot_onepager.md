# Pilot Feedback Platform — One Pager (Clinic Facing)

## Purpose
Provide patients with a transparent, independent feedback channel on prescribed cannabis products — improving trust, retention, and care quality.  

## How It Works (at a glance)
1. **Patient joins pilot**  
   - Simple sign-up via app (no personal data required in public feed).  
   - Consent captured (GDPR compliant).  

2. **Patient leaves review**  
   - Rates quality, consistency, packaging.  
   - Optional photo upload.  

3. **Moderation**  
   - Reviews checked for compliance (no medical advice).  
   - Approved reviews flow to patient feed.  

4. **Clinic insights**  
   - Dashboard shows anonymised, aggregated trends.  
   - Exportable reports for internal use.  

## Benefits for Clinics
- **Patient retention**: Demonstrates transparency, builds trust.  
- **Quality monitoring**: Spot supply issues (e.g., dry flower, poor packaging).  
- **Operational data**: Evidence to support supplier decisions.  
- **Differentiation**: Show patients you listen.  

## Pilot Scope
- **Duration**: 4–6 weeks  
- **Patients**: 20–50 (suggested)  
- **Products**: 2–3 SKUs  
- **Success measures**:  
  - % of patients submitting reviews  
  - Volume & quality of reviews  
  - Reduction in complaints / increase in satisfaction  

## Compliance
- Explicit patient consent captured (GDPR).  
- Reviews are patient opinions only — not medical advice.  
- All reviews moderated with audit trail.

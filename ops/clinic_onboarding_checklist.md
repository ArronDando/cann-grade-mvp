# Clinic Onboarding Checklist (MVP)

- Identify 1-2 pilot clinicians & pharmacy partner
- Confirm non-medical UGC scope & disclaimers
- Staff training: submitting reviews, explaining scope to patients
- Data sharing MOU signed (no patient data shared with platform)
- Dashboard access provisioned; KPIs agreed (retention, churn reasons)
- Weekly check-in: counts, issues, improvements
